# 🎨 Example Voice Prompts

Collection of example voice inputs and their expected enhanced prompts.

## 📝 Simple to Detailed Examples

### Example 1: Simple Object
**Voice Input:**
> "A red apple"

**Expected Transcript:**
```

```

**Expected Enhanced Prompt:**
```

its glossy surface, subtle water droplets, clean white background, food
photography style, high detail, professional quality
```

## 🎯 Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific About Subject
❌ "A car"
✅ "A red sports car on a mountain road"

### 2. Include Setting/Context
❌ "A dog"
✅ "A golden retriever playing in a park at sunset"

### 3. Mention Desired Style (Optional)
❌ "A house"
✅ "A modern house in architectural drawing style"

### 4. Add Mood/Atmosphere
❌ "A forest"
✅ "A mysterious dark forest with fog"

## 🚫 Things to Avoid

### Too Vague
❌ "Something cool"
❌ "A picture"
❌ "Art"

### Too Complex
❌ "A scene with 10 different animals, each doing something different, in various locations..."
(DALL-E handles complexity well, but simplicity often yields better results)

### Conflicting Instructions
❌ "A realistic photo in cartoon style"
❌ "A modern medieval castle"
